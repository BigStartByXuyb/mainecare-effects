## liquid-glass-react 1.1.1

Copyright 2025 MAX ROVENSKY. Licensed under the MIT License.

The displacement-map assets in `glass/src/main/res/raw/` are byte-identical copies of the
maps shipped by liquid-glass-react 1.1.1. The AGSL shader reimplements the package's SVG
filter chain (three-channel `feDisplacementMap`, aberration scales, edge mask) and the
procedural rounded-rect SDF of its `shader` mode. The full MIT text is reproduced in
`third_party/licenses/liquid-glass-react-MIT.txt`.
